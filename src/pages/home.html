Isaac Ahuvia

Isaac Ahuvia is a postdoctoral fellow at the Center for Behavioral Intervention Technologies (CBITs) at Northwestern University and an instructor at Harvard University. His research investigates mental illness identity, the online spaces that shape it, and how to promote healthier understandings of mental health and illness. His research has appeared in publications such as The New York Times, Inside Higher Ed, and Mad in America.
